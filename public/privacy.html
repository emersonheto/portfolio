Privacy Policy – Emerson Gmail Agente

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Emerson Gmail Agente is a personal tool built and used only by Emerson Herrera Tolentino to manage his own Gmail account. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's account is connected to it.

Data accessed

Through the Gmail API, the tool reads, labels, filters and drafts messages in the owner's mailbox. It never sends an email without the owner's explicit confirmation of that specific email.

How data is used and stored

Email content is used only to organize the owner's inbox, summarize messages and prepare reply drafts for him. OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored on devices and servers controlled by the owner. Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Message text may be processed by a language model provider solely to produce those summaries and drafts.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
